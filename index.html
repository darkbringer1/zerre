01 / MEET YOUR NEW DESKTOP NEIGHBOR

Somewhere between a pet, a tiny witness, and a good reason to smile at your screen.

Zerre wanders, naps on the Dock, watches the cursor and reacts when you interact with it. It has moods and little habits of its own. The more days you spend together, the more your patterns show up in how it looks.

Zerre means “speck”, a tiny particle, in Turkish. Small things can grow.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

You do your thing.
Zerre does its little thing.

Connect only the tools you want. Tiny event signals shape Zerre over time. You get a companion with a memory of your days, without another chore to maintain.

03 / CALM BY DEFAULT

A companion that knows when to hush.

Zerre has a small daily nudge budget. It stays quiet during focus time, at night, and during meetings when you connect Calendar. Ignore it for a while and it gets sleepy, never punishing.

04 / MADE TO STAY YOURS

It notices that things happened. It does not read your work.

Agent hooks tell Zerre about handoffs, not prompts or code. Calendar access, if you grant it, tells Zerre when you're busy, not what the meeting is. Its history stays on your Mac. The app has no account or usage telemetry. This website counts visits and a few clicks; the details are on the privacy page.

Notes you choose to write on Zerre's board are saved locally so it can show them again.
