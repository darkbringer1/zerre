Zerre 0.1.0

First public release
  • A pixel creature that lives on your desktop: naps on the Dock, hops onto windows, chases your cursor
  • Grows from what you actually do: a sprout for the days you show up, traits it wears for your habits
  • Dozens of pastimes and trait combos, from juggling to fishing off a window's edge
  • Works alongside Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and OpenCode: holds up a note when an agent needs you
  • Calm by default: at most three nudges a day, quiet in meetings, focus and at night
  • Private: notices that things happen, never what they were, and keeps it all on your Mac
